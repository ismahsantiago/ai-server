KICK-0002 (2026-07-25): target user fixed as entrepreneurs, home workers, and small dev teams giving a second life to machines they would otherwise sell off cheap. Reference machine is a reused consumer laptop acting as a headless server. Consequences the whole roster inherits: hardware is heterogeneous and often old, so hardware tiering (Phase H doctor) is load-bearing rather than a nicety; the operator uses a terminal but is not a sysadmin, so no step may assume network engineering, certificate authorities, or router configuration; and every scope decision is judged against 'the laptop I was going to sell now serves models to my home or office in one command'.
